Devices reporting a level count, from a survey of 15 recent ferroelectric analog memories; 10 of them state one, and every plotted count was confirmed directly against its paper's text. Only 2 of 15 report energy per programming pulse, which is why the comparison is drawn on level count; this device switches at 8.2 aJ per pulse at 2 V. The counts here are reported values under each paper's own criterion, which is the point: this work's 8 is an open-loop count under its own measured cycle-to-cycle noise, and Section V shows level count does not predict network accuracy.
