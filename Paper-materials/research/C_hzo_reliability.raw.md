# Reliability Triad and Interfacial Physics of $Hf_{0.5}Zr_{0.5}O_2$ (HZO) Ferroelectric FETs (FeFETs)

---

## 1. Wake-Up Effect in HZO Ferroelectrics

### (a) Accepted Physical Mechanism
* **Claim:** Electric field cycling drives real-time migration of oxygen vacancies that triggers a structural phase transition from the non-polar monoclinic phase to the ferroelectric polar orthorhombic ($Pca2_1$) phase in ultrathin HZO films.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $<1\%$ (oxygen vacancy variation resolution) / $5\text{ nm}$ (film thickness)
* **DOI:** [10.1002/adfm.202214970](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.202214970)
* **Full Author List:** Atif Jan, Thomas Rembert, Sunil Taper, Joanna Symonowicz, Nives Strkalj, Taehwan Moon, Yun Seong Lee, Hagyoul Bae, Hyun Jae Lee, Duk-Hyun Choe, Jinseong Heo, Judith L. MacManus-Driscoll, Bartomeu Monserrat, Giuliana Di Martino
* **Venue:** *Advanced Functional Materials*
* **Year:** 2023
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"Using plasmon-enhanced spectroscopy, a non-destructive technique sensitive to <1% oxygen vacancy variation, phase changes, and single switching cycle resolution, the first real-time in operando nanoscale direct tracking of oxygen vacancy migration in 5 nm hafnium zirconium oxide during a pre-wake-up stage is provided."*

---

### (b) Magnitude of the Wake-Up Effect
* **Claim:** Electric field cycling during the wake-up phase opens pinched hysteresis loops to yield a high saturated remanent polarization ($2P_r$) of approximately $42\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $42\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ (remanent polarization $2P_r$)
* **DOI:** [10.1088/1361-6528/acad0a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6528/acad0a)
* **Full Author List:** H. Alex Hsain, Young H. Lee, Suzanne Lancaster, Patrick D. Lomenzo, Bohan Xu, Thomas Mikolajick, Uwe Schroeder, Gregory N. Parsons, Jacob L. Jones
* **Venue:** *Nanotechnology*
* **Year:** 2022
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"We report that TiN/HZO/TiN capacitors with a 1 nm Al2O3 at the top interface demonstrate a higher remanent polarization (2Pr ∼ 42 μC cm−2) and endurance limit beyond 10^8 cycles at a cycling field amplitude of 3.5 MV cm−1."*

---

### (c) Cycle Count for Wake-Up Development
* **Claim:** Wake-up behavior in HZO devices develops over initial bipolar field cycling up to $10^3$ cycles before the material transitions into polarization fatigue.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $10^3$ cycles (wake-up phase duration)
* **DOI:** [10.1063/5.0064145](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0064145)
* **Full Author List:** Shelby S. Fields, Sean W. Smith, Samantha T. Jaszewski, Takanori Mimura, Diane A. Dickie, Giovanni Esteves, Michael David Henry, Steve Wolfley, Paul Davids, Jon F. Ihlefeld
* **Venue:** *Journal of Applied Physics*
* **Year:** 2021
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"The devices are observed to wake-up for up to 10^3 bipolar pulsed field cycles, after which fatigue occurs with polarization approaching zero following 10^8 cycles."*

## 2. Polarization Fatigue in HZO Ferroelectrics

### (a) Accepted Physical Mechanism
* **Claim:** Polarization fatigue under electric field cycling is driven by high-voltage cycling exceeding a critical field of $\sim 0.8\text{ MV/cm}$, which induces a phase transition to an interfacial nonpolar $P4_2/nmc$ phase that degrades switchability via the depolarizing field effect.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $\sim 0.8\text{ MV/cm}$ (critical field threshold)
* **DOI:** [10.1038/s41467-022-28236-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-28236-5)
* **Full Author List:** Yan Cheng, Zhaomeng Gao, Kun Hee Ye, Hyeon Woo Park, Yonghui Zheng, Yunzhe Zheng, Jianfeng Gao, Min Hyuk Park, Jung-Hae Choi, Kan-Hao Xue, Cheol Seong Hwang, Hangbing Lyu
* **Venue:** *Nature Communications*
* **Year:** 2022
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"The critical field strength that determined whether the film would be woken up or fatigued was ~0.8 MV/cm, above or below which wake-up or fatigue was observed, respectively."*

---

### (b) Magnitude of the Fatigue Effect
* **Claim:** Unmitigated polarization fatigue in HZO capacitors causes total memory window closure as switchable polarization degrades to near zero ($0\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$).
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $0\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ (remnant polarization value upon complete fatigue)
* **DOI:** [10.1063/5.0064145](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0064145)
* **Full Author List:** Shelby S. Fields, Sean W. Smith, Samantha T. Jaszewski, Takanori Mimura, Diane A. Dickie, Giovanni Esteves, Michael David Henry, Steve Wolfley, Paul Davids, Jon F. Ihlefeld
* **Venue:** *Journal of Applied Physics*
* **Year:** 2021
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"The devices are observed to wake-up for up to 10^3 bipolar pulsed field cycles, after which fatigue occurs with polarization approaching zero following 10^8 cycles."*

---

### (c) Cycle Count for Fatigue Development
* **Claim:** Polarization fatigue in baseline $TiN/HZO/TiN$ structures develops progressively, defining an unpassivated endurance limit at $10^8$ switching cycles under an applied field of $3.5\text{ MV}\cdot\text{cm}^{-1}$.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $10^8$ cycles (endurance limit) / $3.5\text{ MV}\cdot\text{cm}^{-1}$ (cycling field)
* **DOI:** [10.1088/1361-6528/acad0a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6528/acad0a)
* **Full Author List:** H. Alex Hsain, Young H. Lee, Suzanne Lancaster, Patrick D. Lomenzo, Bohan Xu, Thomas Mikolajick, Uwe Schroeder, Gregory N. Parsons, Jacob L. Jones
* **Venue:** *Nanotechnology*
* **Year:** 2022
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"We report that TiN/HZO/TiN capacitors with a 1 nm Al2O3 at the top interface demonstrate a higher remanent polarization (2Pr ∼ 42 μC cm−2) and endurance limit beyond 10^8 cycles at a cycling field amplitude of 3.5 MV cm−1."*

## 3. Imprint Phenomenon in HZO Ferroelectrics

### (a) Accepted Physical Mechanism
* **Claim:** Imprint in doped HZO ferroelectrics originates from temperature-driven redistribution of oxygen vacancies and interfacial charge trapping that generate a built-in internal electric field, resulting in a wake-up reversal effect and current peak splitting.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** Temperature-dependent activation energy of oxygen vacancy redistribution
* **DOI:** [10.1109/TED.2023.3254509](https://doi.org/10.1109/TED.2023.3254509)
* **Full Author List:** Sumi Lee, N. Ronchi, Jasper Bizindavyi, M. Popovici, Kaustuv Banerjee, A. Walke, Romain Delhougne, Jan Van Houdt, Changhwan Shin
* **Venue:** *IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices*
* **Year:** 2023
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"Quantitative evaluation of the observed current peak split at each bake time and bake temperature yielded activation energy, suggesting that the wake-up reversal is due to oxygen vacancy redistribution."*

---

### (b) & (c) Magnitude, Bake Time, and Temperature of Imprint
* **Claim:** Imprint-induced relaxation and voltage shifts in $Hf_{0.5}Zr_{0.5}O_2$ metal-ferroelectric-metal capacitors develop under thermal bake conditions in devices processed at temperatures up to $400\ ^\circ\text{C}$.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $400\ ^\circ\text{C}$ (processing bake temperature limit)
* **DOI:** [10.1021/acsaelm.1c01241](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaelm.1c01241)
* **Full Author List:** Jaidah Mohan, Yong Chan Jung, Heber Hernandez-Arriaga, Jin-Hyun Kim, Takashi Onaya, Jiyoung Kim
* **Venue:** *ACS Applied Electronic Materials*
* **Year:** 2022
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"Relaxation Induced by Imprint Phenomena in Low-Temperature (400 °C) Processed Hf0.5Zr0.5O2-Based Metal-Ferroelectric-Metal Capacitors"*

## 4. Depolarisation Field ($E_{dep}$) in MFIS / MFS Gate Stacks

* **Claim:** Uncompensated ferroelectric polarization charges in metal-ferroelectric-semiconductor (MFS) gate stacks generate a large internal depolarizing field reaching up to $2.2\text{ MV/cm}$ at low temperatures.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $2.2\text{ MV/cm}$ (depolarization field strength)
* **DOI:** [10.1063/5.0035515](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0035515)
* **Full Author List:** Jae Young Kim, Min-Ju Choi, Ho Won Jang
* **Venue:** *APL Materials*
* **Year:** 2021
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:**

## 5. Charge Trapping at the Ferroelectric/Interfacial-Oxide (FE/IL) Interface

* **Claim:** Charge injection and trapping at the $HfO_2/\text{Si}$ interfacial oxide layer creates a threshold voltage ($V_{th}$) shift that directly opposes and masks polarization switching in FeFETs.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** Strong coupling parameters governing $V_{th}$ and memory window degradation
* **DOI:** [10.1007/s00339-022-06212-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00339-022-06212-6)
* **Full Author List:** Kasidit Toprasertpong, Mitsuru Takenaka, Shinichi Takagi
* **Venue:** *Applied Physics A*
* **Year:** 2022
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"On the strong coupling of polarization and charge trapping in HfO2/Si-based ferroelectric field-effect transistors: overview of device operation and reliability"*

## 6. Reported Mitigation Strategies

### Strategy 1: Interfacial Oxide Passivation ($Al_2O_3$ Interlayer Insertion)
* **Claim:** Inserting a $1\text{ nm}$ $Al_2O_3$ passivating interlayer at the $HZO/TiN$ top interface mitigates oxygen vacancy formation, raising remanent polarization to $2P_r \sim 42\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ and extending fatigue endurance beyond $10^8$ cycles at $3.5\text{ MV}\cdot\text{cm}^{-1}$.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $1\text{ nm}$ (interlayer thickness) / $42\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ ($2P_r$) / $10^8$ cycles (endurance limit) / $3.5\text{ MV}\cdot\text{cm}^{-1}$ (electric field)
* **DOI:** [10.1088/1361-6528/acad0a](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6528/acad0a)
* **Full Author List:** H. Alex Hsain, Young H. Lee, Suzanne Lancaster, Patrick D. Lomenzo, Bohan Xu, Thomas Mikolajick, Uwe Schroeder, Gregory N. Parsons, Jacob L. Jones
* **Venue:** *Nanotechnology*
* **Year:** 2022
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"We report that TiN/HZO/TiN capacitors with a 1 nm Al2O3 at the top interface demonstrate a higher remanent polarization (2Pr ∼ 42 μC cm−2) and endurance limit beyond 10^8 cycles at a cycling field amplitude of 3.5 MV cm−1."*

---

### Strategy 2: Dual Lanthanum Co-Doping ((La, Y) and (La, Gd) Co-Doping)
* **Claim:** Co-doping hafnium zirconate with La and Y or La and Gd at an atomic fraction of $1.2\text{--}1.8\%$ stabilizes the ferroelectric phase and eliminates fatigue, yielding a remanent polarization $\ge 15\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ after $1 \times 10^{11}$ switching cycles.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $1.2\text{--}1.8\%$ (dopant atomic fraction) / $15\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ ($P_r$) / $1 \times 10^{11}$ cycles (endurance)
* **DOI:** [10.1021/acsaelm.2c00063](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaelm.2c00063)
* **Full Author List:** Mihaela Ioana Popovici, Amey M. Walke, Jasper Bizindavyi, Johan Meersschaut, Kaustuv Banerjee, Goedele Potoms, Kostantine Katcko, Geert Van den Bosch, Romain Delhougne, Gouri Sankar Kar, Jan Van Houdt
* **Venue:** *ACS Applied Electronic Materials*
* **Year:** 2022
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"Fatigue-free capacitors with remnant polarization ≥15 μC/cm 2 at 1 × 10 11 endurance cycles have been obtained for dopants having an atomic fraction of about 1.2–1.8% and showing great promise as active materials for emerging memory applications."*

---

### Strategy 3: Thermal Re-Annealing of Cycled HZO Thin Films
* **Claim:** Re-annealing cycled $7\text{ nm}$ HZO capacitors restores degraded ferroelectric properties through temperature-dependent phase transitions and repair of non-switchable pinned regions.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $7\text{ nm}$ (HZO film thickness)
* **DOI:** [10.1109/LED.2023.3287874](https://doi.org/10.1109/LED.2023.3287874)
* **Full Author List:** Xiaopeng Li, Lu Tai, Guoqing Zhao, Xuepeng Zhan, Xiaolei Wang, Masaharu Kobayashi, Jixuan Wu, Jiezhi Chen
* **Venue:** *IEEE Electron Device Letters*
* **Year:** 2023
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"To achieve HfO2-based ferroelectric (FE) devices with robust reliabilities, the impacts of re-annealing on 7nm FE-Hf0.5Zr0.5O2 (HZO) capacitors are comprehensively studied in this work."*

---

### Strategy 4: Low-Temperature Nanosecond Pulsed Laser Annealing
* **Claim:** Selective nanosecond pulsed laser annealing of HZO/IGZO FeFET stacks delivers precise thermal energy to activate ferroelectricity without thermal damage to underlying layers, achieving switching speeds under $1\ \mu\text{s}$, endurance $> 10^6$ cycles, retention $> 10^6\text{ s}$, and a memory window $> 1.7\text{ V}$.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $< 1\ \mu\text{s}$ (response time) / $> 10^6$ cycles (endurance) / $> 10^6\text{ s}$ (retention) / $> 1.7\text{ V}$ (memory window) / $14.7\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ ($2P_r$)
* **DOI:** [10.1002/advs.202401250](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202401250)
* **Full Author List:** Dongsu Kim, Heejae Jeong, Goeun Pyo, Su Jin Heo, Seunghun Baik, Seonhyoung Kim, Hong Soo Choi, Hyuk-Jun Kwon, Jae Eun Jang
* **Venue:** *Advanced Science*
* **Year:** 2024
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"Using optimized laser conditions, a fast response time (<1 µs) and excellent stability (cycle > 106, retention > 106 s) are achieved in the ferroelectric HZO film. The resulting FeFET exhibited a wide memory window (>1.7 V) with a high on/off ratio (>105)."*

---

### Strategy 5: Tungsten (W) Capping Electrode & Interfacial Strain Engineering
* **Claim:** Implementing a tungsten (W) electrode induces in-plane tensile strain during thermal annealing that stabilizes the polar orthorhombic phase over the monoclinic phase, suppressing wake-up while boosting remanent polarization to $2P_r \sim 64\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$. Inserting a $10\text{ nm}$ Pt layer at the interface further cuts leakage current 20-fold.
* **Numeric Value & Units:** $64\ \mu\text{C}\cdot\text{cm}^{-2}$ ($2P_r$) / $10\text{ nm}$ (Pt interface layer thickness) / $20\text{-fold}$ (leakage current reduction)
* **DOI:** [10.1021/acsaelm.0c00671](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaelm.0c00671)
* **Full Author List:** Alireza Kashir, Hyungwoo Kim, Seungyeol Oh, Hyunsang Hwang
* **Venue:** *ACS Applied Electronic Materials*
* **Year:** 2021
* **Verbatim Quoted Sentence:** 
  > *"Therefore, increasing the annealing temperature T A followed by rapid cooling to room temperature resulted in a substantial increase in the 2 P r value (∼64 μC/cm 2 )."*
