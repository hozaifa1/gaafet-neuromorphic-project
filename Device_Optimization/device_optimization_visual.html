Turn-by-Turn

The Optimization Journey

Each turn probed one design knob. Changes were kept only if the figure-of-merit improved — coordinate descent with Pareto gating.

Window Trajectory

Memory window (ID_on/ID_off) after each optimization turn
Turn 1 — V_read
Finding the Integration-Preserving Read Point
Deep reads (−0.9 V) inflate DR to 49,000× but destroy integration — read-disturb depolarises the staircase. Only V_read = −0.5 V preserves graded integration (igain = 1.43). High DR must be engineered, not biased.
V_READ = −0.5 V LOCKED
Turn 2 — T_ox
The Central Lever: Interfacial Oxide
Scaling T_ox from 2.0 → 1.0 nm: window explodes 2.6 → 5,800 (2,200×), off-state drops 3,000×, V_op roughly halves. Stronger FE→channel coupling is the dominant FeFET design freedom.
T_OX = 1.0 nm KEPT
Turn 3 — T_si
Thin Body for Gate Control
Thinning the nanosheet from 15 → 5 nm: window climbs 5,800 → 47,309 (8×). The gate gains full electrostatic control over the fully-depleted body → steeper SS → off-state crushed to 3.7×10⁻⁹. On-state even rises slightly.
T_SI = 5 nm KEPT
Turn 4 — N_sub
Doping: Null Result (Floor-Limited)
In the 5 nm fully-depleted body, doping barely matters — the gate controls everything. ID_off is at the solver floor (~3.5×10⁻⁹). The 47k–50k spread is floor noise. ≥5×10¹⁷ collapses on-current.
N_SUB = 10¹⁶ UNCHANGED
Turn 5 — T_fe
FE Thickness: Energy-Optimal Sweet Spot
T_fe = 7 nm peaks the window at 96,240 (2× over 10 nm), drops V_op to 2.5 V, and achieves lowest energy/spike. Below 7 nm the off-state rises — past the coupling/charge optimum.
T_FE = 7 nm KEPT
Turn 6 — L_gate / L_ov
Geometry Lock Confirmation
L_gate = 50 nm → 6× worse (SCE). L_ov = 0 → 6× worse (S/D edge leakage). The overlap gate depletes the S/D edge — beneficial. Geometry fully locked.
GEOMETRY LOCKED
Turn 7 — Pulse/Read Protocol
The Read-Point Correction & LIF Discovery
Short 30 ns reads eliminate read-disturb (igain → 1.0), doubling the window to 224k. The true erase/program window opens at V_G = 0 V (not −0.5 V which sits in GIDL). True window ≈ 3,140× at V_G = 0 V read.
V_READ = 0 V, TRUE WINDOW 3140×
Turns 8–9 — LTP & Robustness
Analog Synaptic Weight & Retention
Cumulative +2 V / 0.3 µs pulses produce 15 distinguishable conductance levels over ~4 decades (LTP). Retention holds flat over 100 µs — non-volatile. Energy: ~0.014 fJ/pulse.
15-LEVEL LTP, NON-VOLATILE
