Tier A interpretation:
  Devices have different geometries (10 nm HZO GAA vs 5 nm ZrO2 planar L=8 um).
  Magnitudes (I_on, MW) differ by physics — NOT calibration error.
  Theoretical max MW = 2*F_c*t_FE = 2.4 V (TCAD stack) vs 1.2 V (Tasneem).
  Calibration is on PHYSICS SHAPE (SS, hysteresis topology) after V_t-align.
  R^2 reported above reflects shape match in normalized subthreshold region.
