Phase 1D — publication figure captions (drop-in)

fig_calibration_aligned.png  (Tier-A MAIN calibration figure)
  Subthreshold I_D/W vs (V_G - V_t) for the GAA-FeFET (this work, solid)
  and the planar Si-channel FeFET of Tasneem et al. 2022 (dashed, Fig. 3c).
  V_G axis shifted by each curve's V_t at I/W = 1 µA/µm; Tasneem's p-type
  V_G axis is flipped for n-type alignment. TCAD post-PGM SS = 100
  mV/dec matches Tasneem's stated 110 mV/dec; post-ERS branch is steeper
  due to single-domain Preisach asymmetry (documented in Methods).

fig_calibration_raw.png  (Supplementary)
  Raw I_D/W vs V_G for both works without V_t alignment. The ~1.2 V offset
  between the n- and p-type devices and the difference in subthreshold
  region reflect the geometry difference (10 nm HZO GAA L_g=100 nm vs
  5 nm ZrO2 planar L_g=8 µm); the per-µm-perimeter normalization is
  invariant to AreaFactor.

fig_memory_window_bar.png  (Tier-A DIFFERENTIATOR figure)
  Memory window ΔV_t = 1.40 V for the 10 nm HZO GAA-FeFET, 2.8×
  the 0.50 V reported for the 5 nm ZrO2 planar baseline (Tasneem
  2022). Theoretical maximum 2·F_c·t_FE = 2.40 V for our stack vs 1.20 V
  for Tasneem's, so the scaling is fully physics-consistent.

fig_ss_bar.png  (Tier-A SS panel)
  Post-PGM SS = 100 mV/dec (within ±20% of Tasneem's 110), post-ERS
  SS = 164 mV/dec (asymmetry from negative-polarization channel
  depletion vs positive-polarization accumulation). Green band = ±20% of
  Tasneem's stated 110 mV/dec.
